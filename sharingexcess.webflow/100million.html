HOW IT WORKS

We make it easy to feed your community

Your donation will directly fund the delivery of fresh and healthy food to local community partners and distribution sites.

1. You sponsor a truck

The food is always free; we just need to fund transportation. Your gift covers the cost of rescuing and delivering surplus food.

2. We source food donations

Our teams recover fresh, high-quality food directly from produce wholesalers, farmers, and major food businesses experiencing surplus.

3. We deliver to the people

That food is distributed to food banks, pantries, shelters, and community partners across our network, reaching hundreds of families in need.

📣 When you donate, we’ll send you a shareable graphic 🧑‍🎨

You’ll receive a graphic you can post or share to show the impact you helped make.

WHY DONATE?

97,179,957 lbs.

We're ~ this close ~ to reaching our goal

This year, we set an ambitious goal to rescue and redistribute 100 million pounds of surplus food across the country. Thanks to an incredible community of partners and supporters, we’ve already delivered over 97 million pounds of food to families, pantries, shelters, and community organizations. As we head into the holidays, we’re just 3 million pounds short of the finish line.

Every truckload brings us closer to meeting this goal. With your help, we can close the gap before the end of the year and make sure food reaches people, not landfills.

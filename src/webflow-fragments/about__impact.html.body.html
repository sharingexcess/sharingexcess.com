Our Impact

Under Fiscal Sponsorship

From 2018-2022 Sharing Excess was under fiscal sponsorship, allowing us to start making impact while we set up our 501c3. Three cheers to the Federation of Neighborhood Centers for their guidance during this time. Financials during this period can be provided upon request.

Increasing access to excess for everyone

What started with extra meal swipes has grown to over 220 million pounds of food delivered to communities and organizations addressing food insecurity across over 40 states.

Every pound of food we rescue is weighed and categorized to accurately measure our impact. From these numbers, we can paint a great picture of impact in terms of meals, monetary value, and environmental benefit. How We Measure Impact

IMPACT HIGHLIGHTS

See what happens when we collaborate for a greater purpose.

Our work doesn’t succeed without thoughtful partnerships with food businesses, non-profits, social justice movements, entertainers, and corporate partners who are all advancing the work for a sustainable & equitable food system. Check out some of our awesome collaborations:
Philadelphia Wholesale Produce Market

Since 2021, we’ve partnered with the Philadelphia Wholesale Produce Market to create a full-scale surplus rescue operation. Together, we’ve rescued over 25 million pounds of fresh food — turning unsold food into fresh food access for communities across the region.

Beast Philanthropy

In partnership with Beast Philanthropy, we’ve moved over 30 million pounds of food — and redefined what large-scale generosity can look like. From surprise warehouse visits to viral video moments, this collaboration continues to raise awareness and feed communities on an unprecedented scale.
Trader Joe's

Every day of the week, across multiple cities and states, Trader Joe’s is helping us rescue perfectly good food from going to waste. By donating fresh surplus from their stores nationwide, they’re proving that corporate partnerships can be both impactful and consistent.
Hunts Point Produce Market

In the heart of the Bronx, we’ve built a thriving food rescue operation at the Hunts Point Produce Market — one of the largest produce markets in the world. Since launching, we’ve rescued over 12 million pounds of food, proving that even the biggest sources of surplus can be part of the solution.
Misfits Market

As supporters of our operation at Hunts Point, Misfits Market is helping power one of the most ambitious food rescue efforts in the country. Their support ensures that surplus food is recovered at the source — and redirected quickly to where it’s needed most.
Tony Robbins Foundation

We’re proud to work with the Tony Robbins Foundation to help close the national meal gap. and expand our work internationally. Their support enables us to scale our work globally and bring millions more meals to families facing hunger.

Financials

Under Fiscal Sponsorship

From 2018-2022 Sharing Excess was under fiscal sponsorship, allowing us to start making impact while we set up our 501c3. Three cheers to the Federation of Neighborhood Centers for their guidance during this time. Financials during this period can be provided upon request.

OUR COMMITMENT

Financial transparency and accountability

At Sharing Excess we are committed to effectively managing our resources for maximum impact. We're a lean team that makes the most out of every dollar donated. Explore our financials to see how we allocate funds and put your support directly towards feeding communities and fighting food waste.

Resources + Documents

Since 2018, Sharing Excess has turned $25 million in funding from 2,500 generous donors into an astounding $375 million worth of retail food cost savings for communities and families in need. That's more than 15X return on investment for every dollar donated.

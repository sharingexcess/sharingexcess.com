University Chapters

Started by students, driven by passion.

Chapters play a crucial role in building food sharing networks. By volunteering, fundraising, and advocating, organizers can work with Sharing Excess to promote food security in their community. Chapters can be powered by college students, high schoolers, or everyday community heroes.

Start a Chapter

THE ROLE OF CHAPTERS

Creating meaningful impact on campuses around the world

63% of food waste happens at the pre-consumer level of the supply chain. Donating food requires time, labor, transportation, and capacity that many food businesses don't have. For this reason, a lot of perfectly great food goes to waste instead of feeding our communities.

Food Rescue

Rescue surplus food from local food businesses with the help of our team and technology. You can track your chapter's impact, engage volunteers, and form a powerful network.

Pop-Up Distributions

Organize and host food distributions on campus and in your community. We'll help you source food and set up so you can distribute and spread the word about SE.

Meal Swipe Donations

Student meal swipes often go to waste. Your chapter can collect unused & donated meal swipes and redirect them to students who may be facing food insecurity on campus.

Advocacy

Be a voice for food security and waste reduction. Engage in educational initiatives and work with stakeholders to create policies that ensure a sustainable and equitable food system on campus.

Fundraising

Create fundraisers that support our mission and your chapter. From marathons to game nights, food drives to concerts, we encourage you to get creative–the sky is the limit!

Volunteering

Lend a helping by volunteering with your chapter. Whether it's with SE, or another organization, you can get people together to make meaningful local impact.

Looking to start a chapter?

Are you ready to be the driving force behind a food rescue revolution in your community? Starting a Sharing Excess chapter is your opportunity to make a lasting impact, create positive change, and transform your passion for reducing food waste into meaningful action.

Creating a chapter is about more than just tackling food waste – it's about building a supportive community of like-minded individuals who share your dedication and enthusiasm.

Don't worry, you won't be alone on this journey. We provide you with the resources, guidance, and support you need to successfully start and run your chapter. From training materials to ongoing mentorship, we're with you every step of the way to ensure your chapter thrives.

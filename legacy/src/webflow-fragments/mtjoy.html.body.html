Get Free Tickets to the Show!

We’re looking for 4 volunteers per city to help collect food and greet fans at the Sharing Excess table. It’s a simple way to make a big impact, and you’ll get free tickets to the show as a thank-you.

🙋  Volunteers receive:
Free ticket to the show (if selected)
🚙  What they need:
A vehicle to transport donations to local food banks
🕑  Time commitment:
About 2 hours (1 hour before doors open)
